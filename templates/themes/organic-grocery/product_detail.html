Storage & Freshness Care Guidelines
Keep Away from Direct Sunlight

Store in a cool, dry pantry shelf away from heating stoves or windows. UV light deteriorates raw unrefined essential oils.

Always Use a Dry Spoon

Do not introduce wet utensils into raw honey jars, cold-pressed oils, or spice tins.

Tightly Close Container Lid
