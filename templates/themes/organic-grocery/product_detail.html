Storage & Freshness Care Guidelines
Keep Away from Direct Sunlight

Store in a cool, dry pantry shelf away from heating stoves or windows. Ultraviolet light deteriorates raw unrefined essential oils.

Always Use a Dry Spoon

Do not introduce wet utensils into raw honey jars, cold-pressed oils, or ground spice tins. Even minor moisture ingress triggers natural fermentation.

Tightly Close Container Lid

Seal the container immediately after every use. Aromatic terpenes in single-origin spices and pure cow ghee evaporate if exposed to air.

Natural Shelf Stability

Best enjoyed within 6-12 months of packaging. Pure raw honey never expires when stored properly in an airtight container.
