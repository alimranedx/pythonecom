Morning Ritual (AM)
  1. Dispense 2 to 3 drops or a dime-sized amount onto clean palms.
  2. Gently press and pat across the face and neck until fully absorbed—do not rub vigorously.
  3. Follow with your favorite lightweight moisturizer to lock in hydration.
  4. Always finish your AM routine with broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen.
Evening Ritual (PM)
  1. Double cleanse to remove daytime impurities, sunscreen, and makeup.
  2. Apply toner or essence to balance pH levels.
  3. Smooth formula generously over cleansed skin, paying extra attention to dry or sensitized areas.
  4. Seal overnight with a nourishing ceramide barrier night cream or sleeping mask.
